I'm Rakhimzhan Baurzhanuly — a Multimedia Technology student at Asia Pacific University in Kuala Lumpur, specializing in Virtual & Augmented Reality. I sit at the intersection of engineering and fine art, building immersive experiences that captivate, educate, and push into new territory.

My work spans 3D artistry in Blender, Maya & 3ds Max, game development in Unity with C#, motion & interactive media in Adobe Animate, and UI/UX design in Figma. I believe the best digital experiences feel inevitable — effortless craft hiding complex thought.
